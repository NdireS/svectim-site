FAQ

Questions we are often asked

Something else on your mind? Email svectim@outlook.com.

Is our data safe with you?+

We follow Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019, sign a data agreement before we touch your records, and never use your data to train other systems. For the most sensitive work we install AI on your own premises, so the data never leaves your building.

Does private AI really work without internet?+

Yes. We run open AI models on a computer in your office and set them up on your documents. Staff can ask questions, summarise files and draft letters during an outage. We add backup power so it keeps running through power cuts. It is not as capable as the largest online services, and we will tell you plainly what it can and cannot do.

What happens in a pilot?+

We pick one problem from your free scan, agree one number to improve, and set up the fix in about two weeks. At the end we compare before and after. If we missed the target, we refund the setup fee. If it worked, you can move to a monthly plan.

How do we pay?+

By M-Pesa or bank transfer. Setup fees are paid up front and monthly plans are billed at the start of each month. Prices are agreed in writing before any work starts.

Is your course certificate a recognised qualification?+

No. Our short courses give a certificate of completion, which shows what you learned. It is not a formal qualification from a national awarding body.

Do you work outside Nairobi?+

Yes. We work across Kenya. Most automation work can be set up remotely, and we travel for on-site installations and staff training.

Can you audit an AI system for us?+

We can review AI systems built by others and help you write AI policies. We will not give an independent review of a system SVECTIM built, and we do not issue formal certification. For that we refer you to an accredited certification body.
