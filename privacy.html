Privacy notice

How we handle your data

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

SVECTIM Ltd is a company in Nairobi, Kenya. We decide how the personal data collected on this website is used, which makes us its data controller under Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019. You can reach us at svectim@outlook.com.

What we collect

  • From the free AI scan form: your name, organisation, email, phone or WhatsApp number (optional), sector and your message.
  • From the course interest form: your name, phone or WhatsApp number, email (optional) and the course you chose.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange the call or scan you asked for.
  • To tell you the start date and payment details of the course you registered for.

We use your details only for the purpose you gave them for. We do not add you to a mailing list, sell your data or share it for marketing. Reply STOP at any time and we will not contact you again.

Who else handles it

  • Our email provider, which stores the messages we receive.
  • A form service that passes form entries to our inbox.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. Both receive your device's IP address when you open a page.

Some of these providers store data outside Kenya. We use only providers that protect data to a standard comparable to Kenyan law.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 12 months after our last contact with you, then delete them, unless you become a client. Client records are kept for as long as the law requires for business and tax records.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act 2019 you may ask us to:

  • tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • stop using your data, including for any contact from us.

Email svectim@outlook.com and we will reply within 7 working days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke.

Changes

If we change this notice we will update the date at the top of this page.
